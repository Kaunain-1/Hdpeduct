Leading provider of engineered piping solutions for infrastructure, water, and telecommunications

Our Story

Hdpeduct was established with a vision to provide high-quality piping solutions for various industries. Over the years, we have grown to become one of the leading suppliers of pipe products, fittings, and telecom equipment in the region.

Our commitment to quality, innovation, and customer satisfaction has earned us the trust of hundreds of clients across Saudi Arabia and beyond. We continue to expand our product range and improve our services to meet the evolving needs of our customers.
